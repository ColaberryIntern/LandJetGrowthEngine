When to use which

Move {name} to this campaign
The person you're looking at belongs in a different bucket. The AI message rewrites in the new campaign's voice. Sequence resets to step 1. Example: DTS.edu in Sports & Events → Education.
Show me the next person in {campaign}
You're done with this person but want to keep working a different campaign. This person stays where they are. You jump to the next available lead in the campaign you picked.
