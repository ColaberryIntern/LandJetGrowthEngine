Outreach Queue: Remove + Block

LandJet Growth Engine update · May 8, 2026 · for Ryan Landry

Ryan,

You flagged that hitting Skip on a lead wasn't getting them out of the queue. You were right. Skip only defers someone for 24 hours -- by the next day they're back. There was no permanent Remove option until today. Now there is.

1. The three buttons, side by side

You'll see them on every card in the Outreach queue, next to the Approve & Send button.

Skip
Defers 24 hours.
They show up tomorrow.
Remove
Out of THIS campaign permanently.
Lead stays in DB, can be re-assigned.
Block
Out of ALL outreach.
Added to do-not-contact list.
Both new buttons confirm before they do anything. You'll get a popup explaining what's about to happen so you can't blow someone away by accident.

2. When to use which

Skip (existing behavior)

  • You're not sure yet. Want to revisit them tomorrow.
  • They're traveling, or you just emailed them about something else.
  • Quick "not today" -- the 24-hour timer takes care of it.

Remove (new -- amber button)

  • The lead is in the wrong campaign (e.g., put in Manufacturing but they're really Banking).
  • Their fit isn't right for THIS campaign's messaging, but they're still a real prospect.
  • You want them gone from this queue but you might want to enroll them somewhere else later.

Block (new -- red button)

  • Spam or wrong-person enrollment. They should never get an email from us, period.
  • Unsubscribe request. They asked us not to contact them.
  • Personal connection or someone we shouldn't ever pitch.

3. How to verify it's working for you

5-minute test (after I tell you it's deployed):
  • Open /outreach
  • Pick the lead that kept reappearing this week
  • Click Remove, confirm the popup
  • Refresh the page -- they should be gone
  • Tomorrow, refresh again -- they should still be gone
  • If you ever change your mind, you can re-assign them on the Leads page
